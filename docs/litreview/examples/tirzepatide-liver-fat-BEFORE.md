## Answer

Tirzepatide has been reported to be beneficial in reducing liver fat content in patients with obesity [1].

## Clinical efficacy

CAP and LSM showed statistically significant between-group differences, with reductions in the TZP + LEKT group compared to the TZP + LCD group [2]. Both groups experienced significant reductions in body weight, with no significant difference between the TZP + LEKT and TZP + LCD groups [2].
