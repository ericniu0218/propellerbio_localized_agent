## Answer

Mast cells have been demonstrated to function as IL-17-producers in both normal and psoriatic human skin [1]. A similar isolation of human lymphocytes yielded 15 clusters, including IL-17 signalling-related CD8 + T cells (CCL20_Tc17, IL-17A_Tc17, IL-17F_Tc17 and IL26_Tc17), based on expression of CD4 , CD8A , or CD8B [2]. Compared to healthy and nonlesional skin biopsies, we found a marked increase of c-Rel expression in psoriatic lesional skin [3]. Of note, the downregulation of c-Rel following etanercept treatment was only seen in patients who successfully responded to the biologic and achieved histological disease resolution, while c-Rel levels remained elevated in non-responders [3]. Previously, our research demonstrated that in patients with psoriasis, UBE2L3 is predominantly downregulated in the epidermis, with the exception of the dermis [2]. The cutaneous phenotype of Ube2l3 △Epi murine models exhibited notable erythema, thickness, and scales as the induction time by tamoxifen (TAM) was prolonged [2]. Reductions in IL-17A, IL-17F, and IL-12B mRNA were observed as early as 2 weeks and approximately 70% normalization of lesional gene expression after 4 weeks [4]. Tildrakizumab is an immunoglobulin G1-kappa monoclonal antibody that inhibits the IL-23/IL-17 pathway and has demonstrated through two three-part randomized Phase 3 clinical trials (reSURFACE 1 and reSURFACE 2) and their extension trials to be an efficacious and safe therapy for the targeted treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque-type psoriasis [5]. Most adverse events were mild to moderate in severity, with no drug-related serious adverse events reported [6]. The maximum proportion of patients achieving Investigator's Global Assessment score of 0 or 1 was 100% in the 3 QX004N cohorts [6].

### IL17A (IL-17, IL-17A, γδT cells secreting IL-17A)

Mast cells have been demonstrated to function as IL-17-producers in both normal and psoriatic human skin [1]. ### NF-κB c-Rel

*1 paper(s). Evidence (papers): human clinical 1, in vivo 1. Outcomes (papers): improved 1, worsened 1.*

In psoriatic lesions, c-Rel expression was markedly increased compared to healthy and nonlesional skin [3]. Downregulation of c-Rel following etanercept treatment was observed only in patients who responded to the biologic [3]. In IMQ-treated WT mice, expression of IL-1β, IL-17A, and IL-17F was increased compared to IMQ-treated c-Rel KO mice [3]. In c-Rel KO mice, IMQ-induced acanthosis was substantially reduced compared to WT mice [3].

### UBE2L3 (Ube2l3)

*1 paper(s). Evidence (papers): human clinical 1, in vivo 1. Outcomes (papers): improved 1, no effect 1.*

UBE2L3 is predominantly downregulated in the epidermis, with the exception of the dermis, in patients with psoriasis [2]. In Ube2l3 △Epi mouse models, the cutaneous phenotype exhibited notable erythema, thickness, and scales, which increased with tamoxifen induction [2]. The proportion of proliferating basal cells and cycling spinous cells increased in the Ube2l3 △Epi mouse epidermis compared to the control epidermis [2]. Reduction of UBE2L3 in keratinocytes suppresses epidermal IL-17 production in mouse Vγ2 + γδT cells [2].

## Disease biology

A similar isolation of human lymphocytes yielded 15 clusters, including IL-17 signalling-related CD8 + T cells (CCL20_Tc17, IL-17A_Tc17, IL-17F_Tc17 and IL26_Tc17), based on expression of CD4 , CD8A , or CD8B [2]. Subsequently, the HIP administration was evaluated in vivo for ameliorating IMQ-induced psoriasis-like symptoms [7]. Expression of nf-Cfl1 resulted in the complete absence of CTMCs [1]. Because ex vivo Th17 polarising conditions required the addition of exogenous IL-6 and IL-1β, we repeated our experiments under Th0 polarising conditions to better observe the intrinsic ability of WT and c-Rel deficient BMDCs to induce Th17 differentiation [3]. IFN and IL-12 pathway genes were normalized [8]. Based on these results, we speculated that TA inhibits PDI by binding to the b’ domain [9]. Biologic agents that target specific cytokines have shown to be effective therapies in the treatment of plaque-type psoriasis over other conventional treatments such as systemic retinoids [5]. A modeling approach that employs the zero-inflated beta distribution was used to account for the bounded nature and distributional characteristics of the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) score data [10]. Another group was defined as cytotoxic T cells (Tc), which were identified by the presence of GNLY , GZMH , HSPA1B and IFNG [2]. The nuclear receptor retinoid-related orphan receptor gamma t ( RORγt, RORC ) has been identified as a pivotal regulator of the differentiation and expansion of Th17 cells  [2].

## Human clinical evidence

c-Rel is highly expressed in lesional skin of patients with psoriasis and TLR7-induced psoriatic lesions in mice [3]. Tildrakizumab is an immunoglobulin G1-kappa monoclonal antibody that inhibits the IL-23/IL-17 pathway and has demonstrated through two three-part randomized Phase 3 clinical trials (reSURFACE 1 and reSURFACE 2) and their extension trials to be an efficacious and safe therapy for the targeted treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque-type psoriasis [5]. PASI 100 demonstrated a dose response at all doses, with 17 patients (33%) achieving PASI 100 with zasocitinib, 30 mg [11]. The maximum proportion of patients achieving Investigator's Global Assessment score of 0 or 1 was 100% in the 3 QX004N cohorts [6]. The proportion of patients receiving QX004N who achieved PASI 75 at week 12 and PASI 90 (90% improvement in PASI) at week 16 in the 150-mg, 300-mg, and 600-mg cohorts was 100%, significantly higher than that in the placebo cohorts (33.3%) [6]. UBE2L3 is predominantly downregulated in the epidermis, with the exception of the dermis [2]. Stimulation of CD3⁺ T cells isolated from human psoriatic epidermis (human epidermal CD3⁺ T cells) with recombinant human CXCL16 (rhCXCL16) increased the percentage of IL-17A-secreting cells, particularly among CD8⁺ T cells [2]. A total of 170 of the 212 randomized participants completed the 12-week treatment period of the study [10]. All model parameters were estimated with acceptable precision [10]. Brepocitinib is an oral selective dual TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor and based on its cytokine inhibition profile is expected to provide therapeutic benefit in the treatment of plaque psoriasis [10].

## Negative and conflicting results

Approximately 41 patients (16%) had received previous treatment with biologics [11]. Biopsies were taken from nonlesional and lesporial skin at baseline and weeks 2 and 4 [4]. Changes in the psoriasis transcriptome and genes induced by IL-17 in keratinocytes were evaluated with microarray profiling and reverse transcriptase-PCR [4]. Patients (n = 30) with moderate-to-severe psoriasis were randomized to once-daily 30 mg (n = 14) or 100 mg (n = 7) PF-06700841 or placebo (n = 9) for 28 days [4]. The IL-23/T helper type 17 cell axis is a target for psoriasis [4]. The investigation of the longitudinal individual PASI profiles indicated no substantial differences overall in the efficacy trajectories between participants who dropped out before the end of the 12-week treatment period and those who completed the 12-week treatment period (Online Resource 2) [10]. Efficacy data from a completed Phase 2a study in patients with moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis were utilized to develop a population exposure-response model that can be employed to inform dose selection decisions for further clinical development [10]. However, the measured sTNFR1 content following TA treatment showed an unexpected decrease, falling even below that of the control group, probably because polyphenols, such as TA, are highly adhesive natural compounds capable of forming conjugates with various macromolecules, particularly proteins []. Polyphenols can precipitate certain alkaloids, peptides, gelatin, and other proteins from solution via hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic and ionic,s, [9].
