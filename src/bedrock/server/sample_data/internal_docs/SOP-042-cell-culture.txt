This SOP describes the standard procedure for mammalian cell culture maintenance in the tissue culture suite, including media preparation, passage schedule, and contamination checks.

Cells must be checked for mycoplasma contamination every 14 days using the approved PCR-based detection kit. Any positive result triggers immediate quarantine of the affected line and notification of the QA lead within 24 hours.

Passage number must not exceed 30 for any research cell line used in a GLP study without documented justification and QA sign-off.
