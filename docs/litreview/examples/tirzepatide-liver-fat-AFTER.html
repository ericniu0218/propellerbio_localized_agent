Answer

Evidence basis: [1] substudy of a randomised controlled trial (SURPASS-3), patients, abstract only; [2] post hoc or secondary analysis of a randomised controlled trial (SURPASS-3), patients (n=276), full text; [3] retrospective real-world observational study, patients, full text.

In adults with type 2 diabetes, tirzepatide 10 mg and 15 mg groups showed a greater absolute reduction in liver fat content (-8.09%) at week 52 compared to the insulin degludec group (-3.38%), as measured by MRI-proton density fat fraction.[1] Approximately 30%–40% of tirzepatide-treated participants with baseline liver fat content (LFC) ≥ 6% achieved LFC < 6% at week 52, regardless of the presence of the G allele.[2] Tirzepatide use was significantly associated with a reduced risk of the composite primary outcome compared to thiazolidinedione, with a hazard ratio of 0.66 (95% CI, 0.58–0.42).[3]

Clinical efficacy

Evidence basis: [1] substudy of a randomised controlled trial (SURPASS-3), patients, abstract only.

The pooled tirzepatide 10 mg and 15 mg groups demonstrated a mean reduction in LFC of -8.09% at week 52, compared to -3.38% in the insulin degludec group.[1]

Preclinical efficacy

Evidence basis: [4] animal study, mice (n=32), full text.

Both Sema and TZP reduced hepatic lipid accumulation in mice, as confirmed by Oil Red O staining.[4] HFHFr feeding increased the hepatosomatic index, which was reduced by both Sema and TZP.[4] Serum ALT and AST levels, indicators of hepatocellular injury, were increased in HFHFr-fed mice and were significantly lowered by Sema and TZP.[4] TZP treatment was associated with lower expression of certain genes compared with HFHFr mice.[4]

Mechanism of action

Evidence basis: [4] animal study, mice (n=32), full text.

TZP treatment was associated with reduced hepatic CCL2/CCR2 axis components, and decreased PI3K abundance and lower AKT phosphorylation.[4] Integrated liver transcriptomic-proteomic profiling highlighted chemokine signaling and PI3K-AKT pathway signatures to be associated with TZP.[4] Both Sema and TZP ameliorated these abnormalities.[4] Food intake, assessed every 3 days during the intervention, did not differ significantly among groups, although it varied over the course of treatment.[4]
