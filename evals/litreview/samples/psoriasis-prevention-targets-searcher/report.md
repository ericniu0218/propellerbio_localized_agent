### IL17RA (IL-17RA)

*2 paper(s). Evidence (papers): human clinical 1, in vitro 1, in vivo 1. Outcomes (papers): improved 2, no effect 1. Agents: brodalumab, topical targeting. **Papers disagree on this target.***

Brodalumab, an IL-17RA antagonist, consistently demonstrated the quickest time to relapse, whereas bimekizumab, the first-in-class dual IL-17A and IL-17F antagonist, demonstrated the longest time to relapse [4]. Both compounds achieved efficacy comparable to systemic anti-IL17RA antibody in this model, indicating that topical targeting can recapitulate key features of systemic blockade [3]. Psoriatic inflammation is driven by reciprocal signaling between the epithelial and systemic immune compartments [3]. The convergent suppression of neutrophil effectors, antimicrobial peptides, and inflammatory mediators across mechanistically distinct interventions reinforces IL17RA as a central signaling node orchestrating psoriasiform inflammation [3]. To systematically identify regulators of surface IL17RA expression in HEKa, we developed a FACS-based CRISPR screening approach enabling isolation of two distinct cellular populations representing the top and bottom ~5% of surface IL17RA-expressing cells (Fig. ) [3].

### DGAT2

*1 paper(s). Evidence (papers): human clinical 1, in vitro 1, in vivo 1. Outcomes (papers): improved 1, no effect 1, worsened 1.*

DGAT2 levels were found to be increased in the supernatant of keratinocytes derived from patients with psoriasis compared with healthy controls [6]. Despite reduced intracellular DGAT2 expression, DGAT2 secretion was elevated in psoriatic keratinocytes [6]. The absolute concentration of DGAT2 in the supernatant remained much lower than its intracellular levels [6]. Significant alterations in glycerides, glycerophospholipids, sphingolipids, and fatty acids were observed in Spry1 ΔEpi mice [6]. Downregulation of DGAT2 was validated in the absence of SPRY1 in mice [6]. DGAT2 downregulation and lipid dysregulation enhanced TLR3-mediated inflammatory signaling in keratinocytes [6]. Increased DGAT2 secretion from keratinocytes promoted CD8 + T cell activation, proliferation, and survival [6].
